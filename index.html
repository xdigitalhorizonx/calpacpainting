Preparation is the job

The part you never see is the part you pay for.

Anyone can put color on a wall. What decides whether it still looks right in five years is what happened before the first coat — the wood restoration, the old caulking that came out, the spackling, the sanding, the masking.

That is where our time goes, and it is why our bids read the way they do.

  • Every surface prepared before it receives any finish
  • Approved materials meeting government, builder and association specifications
  • A painting supervisor keeping records of all job-site activity
  • A multi-point quality inspection on completion
  • The job site picked up daily — not just at the end
