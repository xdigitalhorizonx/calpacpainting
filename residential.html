What we do for homeowners

Six services, and the same preparation under all of them.

This is the residential list, start to finish. Every one of them begins the same way — with the surface, not the color.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors — masked, cut in and rolled, with your furniture and floors covered before a lid comes off a can.

Exterior painting

Stucco, siding, fascia, eaves and garage doors, prepared for a climate that is unforgiving on a south-facing wall.

Staining

Doors, trim, beams, gates and other woodwork stained and finished — including the wood restoration that has to happen before any of it.

Wall repair

Cracks, dings, nail pops and tired texture spackled, sanded and blended, so the repair disappears under the finish instead of standing out in low sun.

Pressure washing & cleaning

Exteriors, walkways, block walls and patios washed down — as a job on its own, or as the first step of an exterior repaint.

Color consultation

Getting to a color you will still like in year three. We help homeowners narrow it down and coordinate it room to room before anything is ordered.

Preparation is the job

Most of what you are paying for happens before the color goes on.

Wood restoration. Old caulking cut out rather than painted over. Spackling. Sanding. Masking everything that is not getting painted. None of it photographs well, and all of it decides whether the job still looks right in five years.

It is the least glamorous part of the trade and the only part that determines how a finish ages in this climate.

  • Wood restoration before anything receives a finish
  • Old caulking removed, not painted over
  • Spackling and sanding until the wall reads flat
  • Masking and covering — furniture, floors, fixtures and glass
  • Only approved materials, supplies, equipment and the finest paints
  • The job site picked up daily, so you get your house back each evening

Before you book

Before you book the crew.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Tell us what needs painting and we will come out and walk the house with you at no charge. Call (702) 383-5144 or send the details to calpacpaintingnv@calpac.us and we will set a time.

How long does a repaint take?

It depends on the house, on how much preparation the surfaces actually need, and on whether you are doing the interior, the exterior or both — which is what the walk-through is for. What we can tell you is that our painting supervisor keeps a record of all job-site activity and follows it through, so the schedule you are given is the schedule the job is run to.

What happens to my furniture and floors?

Masking and covering is part of the preparation, not something we get to at the end. Furniture, floors, fixtures and anything else that is not being painted is protected before any finish goes on, and our professional staff picks up the job site every day rather than leaving it until the job is finished.

Is the work guaranteed?

Every Cal Pac job carries a one-year workmanship warranty. Before we call a job finished, the painting supervisor completes a multi-point quality inspection, and every client also gets a direct customer service telephone line: (702) 313-7884.

Do you offer financing?

Financing is available. What is on offer depends on the job, so ask your estimator at the walk-through and we will go through it with you rather than quote you something over the phone that does not apply.

Do you have Spanish-speaking painters?

Se habla español. If it is easier to arrange your estimate in Spanish, say so when you call (702) 383-5144 or write to calpacpaintingnv@calpac.us.
