The people who've painted Las Vegas for 35 years.

Cal Pac Painting of Nevada is owner-run from one yard on South Commerce Street. The company is fifty-five years old; most of the faces below have been part of it for over a decade.

The short version

Started in California. Settled in Las Vegas.

Cal Pac Painting opened for business in California in 1971. Sherrie Hermann joined the company in 1984 and invested in it in 1988. By 1991 the work had followed the growth east, into the Las Vegas valley, and in 1996 the company was incorporated here as Cal Pac Painting of Nevada, Inc.

In 2001 the original California location was sold so the whole business could point at one market. It has been pointed at this one ever since — one office on South Commerce Street, one set of crews, one standard.

Whether you're a general contractor putting up a commercial building, a management company repainting an apartment complex, or a homeowner redoing the house you live in — Cal Pac is set up for it.

Leadership

Two people run this company, and both of them still work.

No holding company, no regional manager three states away. The people who run Cal Pac are the people who answer for it.

Sherrie Hermann — President & CEO

Sherrie joined Cal Pac in 1984, invested in the company in 1988, and has led it ever since. She made the call to sell the California location in 2001 and put the entire company behind Las Vegas — which is why a valley customer deals with the owner's company rather than a branch of one.

Greg Combs — Executive Vice President

Forty-two years in the painting trade, twenty of them running Cal Pac's field operations. Crews, schedules and what actually happens on the site are his department.

Community service

The valley is also where we live.

None of this shows up in a bid, and none of it makes a wall straighter. It is simply where a fifty-five-year-old Las Vegas company puts some of its time.

Rebuilding Together Southern Nevada

The volunteer effort that repairs and refreshes homes for valley residents who can't take the work on themselves. Cal Pac takes part.

Dress for Success

Work clothing, coaching and a way back into employment for women who need one. Cal Pac supports the program.

Girl Scouts of Southern Nevada

Cal Pac supports the Girl Scouts of Southern Nevada and its programs for girls across the valley.

Take Your Child to Work Day

Cal Pac takes part — one day a year the crew's kids get to see what a painting contractor actually does all day.

SNHBA Spring Builders Golf Classic

The annual golf outing for Southern Nevada's home-building industry — the builders, subcontractors and suppliers who put this valley up.

Boy Scout Eagle award

Cal Pac's community record includes a Boy Scout Eagle award — one more thing that has nothing to do with painting and everything to do with sticking around.
