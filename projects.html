A bank branch and a high-rise tower want different things from a painter.

Each of these is a different schedule, a different specification and a different set of people to keep out of the way of. The names under each one are real jobs.

Financial & retail

Bank of America, Capital One and Chase Bank branches; Brooks Brothers at the North Premium Outlets; The Jewelers on Western Avenue; és Footwear. Branded exteriors, tight trading hours.

Schools & institutional

Green Valley High School and Parsons Elementary School, both new door packages. Institutional jobs get finished to the government and association specifications the building is held to.
