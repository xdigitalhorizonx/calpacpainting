Before the bid package

What general contractors and property managers ask us first.

How large a commercial project can Cal Pac bid?

Cal Pac carries a $750,000 commercial bid limit, which covers most stand-alone buildings, retail plazas and office complexes in the valley. We are also set up for small work — a single suite, one building elevation or a lot re-stripe are all normal jobs for us.

Are you licensed in Nevada?

Yes. Cal Pac Painting of Nevada, Inc. holds Nevada State Contractors Board license number 0044823. The company opened in California in 1971, has painted the Las Vegas valley since 1991 and was incorporated in Nevada in 1996. If your bid package needs current licensing or insurance documentation, our office at (702) 383-5144 will put it together for you.

Can you work around an occupied building?

That is most of what we do. The sequence is set at the walk-through, a painting supervisor keeps a record of all job-site activity while the work runs, and our staff picks up the job site daily rather than at the end of the project — so tenants, residents and customers are not living around a staging area for a month.

What warranty comes with the work?

Every Cal Pac client gets a one-year workmanship warranty. Before that starts, the painting supervisor completes a multi-point quality inspection on the finished job, and every client also gets a direct customer service telephone line: (702) 313-7884.

Do you handle HOA and REO properties?

Yes. HOA and REO painting and compliance is a named part of our commercial work: full-community repaints for boards and property managers, and REO properties brought back into condition before handover.

Do you have Spanish-speaking crews?

Se habla español. Say so when you call (702) 383-5144 or write to calpacpaintingnv@calpac.us, and we will take it from there.
